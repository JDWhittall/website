QUICK HELP

Using this score sheet

Type your guesses as you play. Inkling allows up to seven guesses for each neighbour across the whole game.

After round three, tap Score game. Reveal clue cards and mark each guess as wrong, 1, 2, or 3 points.

Your two guessing scores are calculated automatically. For each ink score, ask that neighbour how many points they scored guessing your clue words and enter it here.

Your final score is both guessing scores plus both ink scores. If tied, the player who made fewer guesses wins; if still tied, the victory is shared.
